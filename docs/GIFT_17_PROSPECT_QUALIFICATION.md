## 7. Thesis Attack — six required questions

### 1. Would the likely finding actually be new/useful to an executive?
PASS. A live competitor page currently describes Henry as lacking underwriting, market overview, comps/market data and cloud workflow while current Henry product pages show underwriting, suggested comps, market briefing, database and Excel workflow. Even if Henry knows IntellCRE exists, the exact buyer-facing mismatch is actionable.

### 2. If the founder already knows the issue, does the Gift still have value?
PASS. The value is not merely "IntellCRE is a competitor." The useful output is an evidence-backed map of which external buyer surfaces are describing the old Henry versus Henry Deal, what claims are contradicted, and which reference class buyers may now place Henry into.

### 3. Can the finding improve a real decision?
PASS. It can inform whether Henry should update its own comparison/sales-enablement material, counter specific stale claims, clarify the Henry Deal boundary, and decide how to frame Buildout/other CRE workflow platforms after the product expansion.

### 4. Could evidence plausibly reject the thesis?
PASS. The thesis would collapse if the IntellCRE page were no longer live/current, if Henry's current primary sources did not support the allegedly missing capabilities, or if the broader competitors did not overlap Henry's current buyer/workflow. Finding 01 survived this test. Finding 02 remains bounded. Finding 03 is explicitly not promoted to a finding until verification is complete.

### 5. Are we making the issue sound larger than the evidence supports?
PASS WITH GUARDRAILS. Do **not** claim Henry is unaware of the issue, that IntellCRE materially changes win rate, that Newmark is switching vendors, or that Buildout is a direct one-for-one replacement. The supported claim is narrower: current public buyer surfaces can place Henry in an outdated/narrower reference class than Henry's own current product evidence supports.

### 6. Is there a natural paid follow-on without deliberately crippling the Gift?
PASS. A paid follow-on could map the full external buyer surface by segment, validate competitor/reference-class boundaries across investment sales, debt/capital markets and sponsor/owner-operator use cases, and quantify claim drift across comparison/review/discovery surfaces. The Gift itself still has to deliver the strongest verified finding and enough evidence to act on it.
